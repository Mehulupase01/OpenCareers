## Implemented Measurements

Owner-reviewed relevance datasets freeze exact assessment/job/profile attribution,
minimized labels and a server recording cutoff. Compatible model/provider routes,
canonical-job uniqueness and evidence digests are required. The reports expose
rank correlation and discrimination diagnostics with sample sizes, fixed review
windows and refusal states; they do not produce hiring probabilities. Scoring
code-version and immutable provider-build attribution remain incomplete.

Fixed outcome cohorts qualify typed, digest-checked non-mock confirmed receipts,
deduplicate canonical jobs and exclude synthetic, unverified, unresolved,
outside-window and still-observing applications. Receipt observation time starts
the horizon; only known events occurring by that horizon contribute labels.
UNKNOWN preserves silence. These are current recorded horizon-state fractions,
not cumulative ever-screened/interviewed/offered conversion. Each reported fraction
has numerator, denominator and observation window; exclusions precede grouping
and must not be misrepresented as source-specific exclusions. Minimum sample is
at least 20 independently for each compared group; smaller groups refuse rates.

Reviewed experiment variants bind before intent/dispatch to one existing packet
and canonical job. Frozen attribution requires matching assignment, definition,
approval and packet hashes, an intent carrying that assignment, and monotonic
assignment/intent/dispatch/receipt timestamps. Stopping does not rewrite history.
Only distinct groups from one definition at its declared observation horizon can
be compared. Differences are descriptive and preserve each denominator and
minimum-sample refusal. Manual assignment is not randomization.
